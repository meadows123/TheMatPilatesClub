Meet Susie

My journey

How Pilates found me

Pilates found me while I was living in Canada. What started as a class I tried quickly became the part of my week I looked forward to most. I loved how it challenged me, and how I always left feeling clearer, stronger and more like myself.

Back in the UK, I wanted to understand the why behind every movement, not just the how. Training as an instructor changed the way I see the body: every exercise has a purpose, and no two people move in quite the same way.

Now, teaching is my favourite part of the week. Whether you're brand new or have practised for years, my hope is simple: that you leave every class feeling stronger, calmer and proud of what your body can do.

Susie x
