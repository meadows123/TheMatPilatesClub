About the Club

Pilates that meets you where you are

The Mat Pilates Club is a small, friendly space for mat-based Pilates. No reformers, no intimidation, just thoughtful movement, good company and an hour that's yours.

01

Small classes

Limited spaces so every person gets real attention, form corrections and options that suit their body.

All levels welcome
