Meet your instructor

Susie

Founder & Pilates Instructor

Susie is a STOTT PILATES® certified Matwork instructor and the founder of The Mat Pilates Club. Her Pilates journey began in Canada, training at some of the country's leading Pilates studios, before completing her instructor training with STOTT PILATES® in London, recognised as a gold standard in Pilates education.

Her teaching is rooted in anatomy, alignment and the science of human movement, blending the foundations of classical Mat Pilates with a modern, contemporary approach. Expect a welcoming class with a strong focus on control, breath and precision, and every exercise adapted to suit your body.

Susie's passion lies in helping people build strength, confidence and a deeper connection to their bodies, in small, supportive classes where everyone is seen and encouraged, whether it's your first class or your fiftieth.
