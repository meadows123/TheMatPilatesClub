Good to know

Questions

I've never done Pilates. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Every class includes options for beginners, and the trial pass is designed for exactly this.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, a water bottle, and a mat if you have one. Spare mats are available.

How do I book?

Head to the Schedule & Booking page, pick a class and choose your pass. It takes less than a minute.

I have an injury or I'm pregnant. Can I join?

Please get in touch before your first class so exercises can be adapted for you safely.
