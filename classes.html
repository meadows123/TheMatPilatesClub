Hot Mat Challenge

An intermediate hot mat class using infrared heaters, which warm your muscles directly to boost flexibility and circulation while you work. Expect a challenging full-body workout focused on core strength, stability and powerful, strength-based movements.

Faster movements raise the intensity while you keep control and flow, building on the fundamentals you know. Equipment will often be used to take your workout further.

Good to know: a hot mat towel is included, socks must be worn at all times, and please arrive 5 minutes early, as the door is locked once class starts. If you ever feel unwell, dizzy or nauseous, stop, leave the room and let your instructor know straight away. Booked through Elevate.

Good to know

Questions

I've never done Pilates. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Every class includes options for beginners, and the Intro Pack is designed for exactly this. If you'd like a gentler start, the Private Start Pack begins with a one-to-one session.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, please book ahead. Classes are kept small, so spaces are limited and often fill up. You can see upcoming classes and book on the Booking page.

What happens if a class is full?

Join the waiting list and we'll contact you straight away if a space opens up. Spaces go to the waiting list in the order people joined.

Can I switch my booking if something comes up?

Of course. Just message us on Instagram or by email at least 24 hours before your class and we'll move you to another class at no cost.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel at least 24 hours before your class and it goes straight back onto your pack. Late cancellations and no-shows count as a used class, as that space could have gone to someone on the waiting list.

What should I bring?

Just water! Mats are included. Wear comfortable clothes you can move in.

How do I book?

Tap any Book button or head to the Booking page. Bookings and payments are handled securely through Bookwhen and take less than a minute.

I have an injury or I'm pregnant. Can I join?

Please get in touch before your first class so exercises can be adapted for you safely.
